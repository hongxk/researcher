个人简介

About

上海交通大学博士、访问学者，福建省引进高层次人才。从事基于计算系统生物学的蛋白质动态结构与互作网络研究，以及在酶催化机理与药物筛选等领域的应用，包括PTM功能效应预测、蛋白聚集调控及靶标构象变化模拟等。目前已在Advanced Science、Chemical Engineering Journal、Green Chemistry和Journal of Hazardous Materials等国际知名期刊发表SCI论文25篇（中科院1区12篇，中科院2区8篇）；授权2项发明专利和5项软件著作权；主持国家自然科学基金青年科学基金、福建省人社厅高层次人才和青年优秀人才培养资助项目、福州大学新进教师科研启动基金等项目。

数据库

PTMint

PTMint is a manually curated database of experimental evidence for PTMs modulating protein-protein interactions across multiple organisms, integrating 2,477 PTM sites and 2,371 PPI pairs associated with 357 diseases. It provides comprehensive annotations to support understanding of PTM-mediated PPI regulation in disease and drug discovery.

PhosPPI

PhosPPI is a sequence-based machine learning predictor for phosphorylation effects on PPIs, requiring no structural input and achieving superior accuracy and AUC. Users submit protein sequences and phosphorylation sites to obtain predictions of enhanced, inhibited, or no effect.

磷酸化 机器学习 PPI
访问工具
数据库

PTMPhaSe

PTMPhaSe is a curated database of PTM regulation on liquid-liquid phase separation (LLPS), covering 733 PTM sites in 164 proteins across 14 organisms and integrating sequence, structural, and PPI annotations. It also offers human proteome-scale predictions of functional phosphorylation sites regulating LLPS, aiding research in PTM-regulated LLPS and disease.

预测工具

PhosLLPS

PhosLLPS is a Transformer-based graph neural network predictor for identifying phosphorylation sites that regulate LLPS, achieving an AUC of 0.9116. Users can input protein sequences and designated sites to obtain rapid predictions, complementing the PTMPhaSe database.
